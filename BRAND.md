## Signature

The proprietary gesture is the cyan directional stroke crossing the M:
- full visual weight, comparable to the M strokes;
- diagonal movement from lower-left to upper-right;
- a restrained arrow-like terminal;
- flat geometry only: no glow, shadow or gradient.

The arrow is part of the M221Tech mark, not a separate decorative icon.

## Rules

- Use the horizontal wordmark when the full M221Tech name has enough room.
- Use the signature M alone for compact identity surfaces.
- Keep the arrow cyan and the M neutral; project colors remain project-specific.
- Do not add a tagline inside the canonical logo.
- The `-dark` variants are for light surfaces only.
- Do not recreate the wordmark with live text: use the vector assets so spacing remains controlled.
- Social/share previews use the dedicated 1200×630 raster card.
- Favicon and touch-icon exports use the same directional M signature.

The canonical identity is the SVG arrow-signature system; raster files exist only where platform compatibility or social previews require them.
